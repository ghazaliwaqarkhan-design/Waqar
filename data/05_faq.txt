Q: What is Nangarhar University?
A: Nangarhar University is a public university in Nangarhar Province, Afghanistan. It provides higher education in fields such as medicine, engineering, computer science, agriculture, economics, law, sharia, education, science, and languages.

Q: Where is Nangarhar University located?
A: Nangarhar University is located in Darunta, near Jalalabad city, Nangarhar Province, Afghanistan.

Q: When was Nangarhar University established?
A: Nangarhar University was established in 1962.

Q: Is Nangarhar University public or private?
A: Nangarhar University is a public university.

Q: Does Nangarhar University have Computer Science?
A: Yes. The university has a Faculty of Computer Science.

Q: What can I study in Computer Science?
A: Areas include Computer Science, Software Engineering, and Computer Networks.

Q: Does Nangarhar University have Medicine?
A: Yes, it has a Faculty of Medicine.

Q: Does Nangarhar University have Engineering?

Q: Does Nangarhar University have Agriculture?
A: Yes. It has a Faculty of Agriculture with several agricultural disciplines.
