4. Academic Fields
Nangarhar University has faculties covering many areas of education.
Major academic areas include:
- Medicine
- Engineering
- Computer Science
- Agriculture
- Economics
- Law and Political Science
- Sharia
- Education
- Science
- Languages and Literature
- Veterinary-related and agricultural sciences
- Social sciences
- Natural sciences

5. Faculty of Computer Science
The Faculty of Computer Science provides education in information technology and computer-related fields.
Important areas include:
- Computer Science
- Software Engineering
- Computer Networks
Students study subjects related to:
- Programming
- Software development
- Database systems
- Computer networks
- Operating systems
- Web development
- Algorithms
- Data structures
- Computer architecture
- Information technology
- Cybersecurity
- Systems analysis
Possible career paths include:
- Software Developer
- Web Developer
- Mobile Application Developer
- Database Administrator
- Network Engineer
- System Administrator
- IT Specialist
- Software Engineer
- Cybersecurity Specialist

6. Faculty of Engineering
The Faculty of Engineering covers engineering disciplines such as:
- Civil Engineering
- Architectural Engineering
- Water Resources and Environmental Engineering
Engineering students study subjects related to construction, infrastructure, architecture, water management, environmental systems, engineering mathematics, physics, and technical design.

7. Faculty of Agriculture
Agriculture is an important academic field at Nangarhar University because agriculture plays a major role in Afghanistan’s economy.
Areas include:
- Agronomy
- Animal Sciences
- Agricultural Economics and Development
- Horticulture
- Plant Protection
- Agricultural Business Management
Students can study:
- Crop production
- Soil science
- Plant diseases
- Agricultural economics
- Animal production
- Horticulture
- Agricultural management
- Plant protection
- Agricultural development

9. Faculty of Science

- Chemistry

Students study areas such as:
- Anatomy
- Physiology
- Biochemistry
- Pharmacology
- Pathology
- Microbiology
- Internal medicine
- Surgery
- Pediatrics
- Obstetrics and gynecology
- Public health
Medical education includes theoretical studies and practical/clinical training.

12. Faculty of Law and Political Science
This faculty provides education in:
- Law
- Political science
- Legal systems
- Constitutional studies
- International relations
- Public administration

13. Faculty of Sharia
The Faculty of Sharia provides higher education in Islamic studies and Islamic jurisprudence.
Areas include:
- Fiqh
- Usul al-Fiqh
- Islamic law
- Tafsir
- Hadith
- Islamic studies
- Comparative jurisprudence
Postgraduate studies are also available in areas such as:
- Tafsir and Hadith
- Fiqh and Law

14. Faculty of Languages and Literature
This faculty focuses on languages, literature, communication, and linguistic studies.
Students may study:
- Pashto
- Dari
- Literature
- Linguistics
- Language teaching
- Literary studies
