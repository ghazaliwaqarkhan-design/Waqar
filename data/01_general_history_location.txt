Nangarhar University is one of the major public universities in eastern Afghanistan. It provides higher education in a wide range of academic fields including medicine, engineering, agriculture, computer science, economics, law, Islamic studies, education, science, and languages.

2. History
Nangarhar University was established in 1962 in Nangarhar Province.
The university initially developed around medical education and later expanded into many different academic fields.
Over the years, Nangarhar University became one of the important higher-education institutions in eastern Afghanistan.
The university has contributed graduates to different sectors of Afghanistan, including government, education, healthcare, engineering, agriculture, business, technology, and other professional fields.

3. Location
Nangarhar University is located in Darunta, near Jalalabad city, Nangarhar Province.
