15. Higher Education
Nangarhar University provides education at different academic levels.
These can include:
- Bachelor’s programs
- Master’s programs
- PhD programs
Availability of specific programs can change depending on university decisions and Ministry of Higher Education policies.

16. Admission
Admission to public universities in Afghanistan is generally connected with the Kankor examination and the admission procedures of the Ministry of Higher Education.
Students normally need to:
1.	Complete the required application.
2.	Participate in the Kankor process when applicable.
3.	Select their desired field/faculty.
4.	Receive their placement.
5.	Complete university registration.
6.	Provide required documents.
Admission requirements and procedures can change, so the chatbot should use the latest official university or Ministry information for current admission questions.

17. Student Registration
After receiving admission, students normally complete university registration.
Registration may involve:
- Identification documents
- Kankor/admission information
- Educational documents
- Photographs
- Registration forms
- Faculty documentation
Exact requirements may differ according to the faculty and current university regulations.

18. Bachelor’s Degree
Bachelor’s education normally consists of several years of academic study depending on the program.
Students must:
- Attend classes
- Complete assignments
- Pass examinations
- Complete practical work when required
- Complete required credits/courses
- Meet graduation requirements

19. Master’s Degree
Nangarhar University also has postgraduate education.
Master’s programs allow students to specialize in a particular academic field after completing undergraduate education.
Admission requirements can include:
- Bachelor’s degree
- Entrance examination or selection process
- Required academic documents
- Other requirements established by the university or Ministry of Higher Education.

20. PhD
The university also has doctoral-level education in selected academic fields.
A PhD is an advanced research degree involving:
- Specialized study
- Research
- Academic supervision
- Dissertation
- Defense
Availability of PhD programs depends on the university’s current postgraduate offerings.
